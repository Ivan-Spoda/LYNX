## 0. Reading her Soundtoys states without decoding them [EXEC]
Her projects hold Soundtoys VST2 chunks (`WIDGET = …; PRESET =<encoded>;`, extracted by round vm124 into
`tmp/vm124/st/*.bin`). The VST3 trial's component state is the same text inside a VST2-compatible wrapper
(`VstW` + an `fxb` bank header with the plugin's VST2 ID). `tools/fxmeasure/py/stwrap.py` puts her chunk text into the
wrapper of a state saved by the trial itself (`savestate`), the host loads it (`loadstate`, i.e. the plugin's own
`setStateInformation`) and reads every parameter back with `get`. All her states were read: Little AlterBoy 52,
Decapitator 63, PanMan 50, EchoBoy 22 (`her/{LittleAlterBoy,Decapitator,PanMan,EchoBoy}.tsv`). PanMan's source,
rhythm note, steps and direction are not host parameters: they were read from the trial's window, one screenshot per
distinct setting (7). LYNX translates these exact states by the FNV-1a hash of the payload
(`src/core/SoundtoysStates.inc`, generated by `tools/fxmeasure/py/gen_st.py`; 17 distinct payloads); any other state
keeps the substitute's default. [EXEC]

Her settings [DATA, EXEC]: Little AlterBoy — Quantize, Formant +1.2 st, Drive 4.6, Mix 100 % (84 % of her slots), an
octave-down Transpose (−12 / −12, drive 5.5), two 44 % mixes. Decapitator — style A everywhere, Drive 1.9–2.0, Tone
+1.7 / +4.2 dB, High Cut 8.1 kHz or off, Mix 88 / 100 %, Auto on, Output trim −3.2 / −3.0 dB. PanMan — "Faast Shimmer
Pan" (LFO 3.56 Hz back and forth, 150.6°, soft, analog: 52 % of slots), "Hard Bounce" (rhythm shape, 1/4), "5 Step"
(5 rhythm steps, 1/4), "Pendulum" (LFO 0.57 Hz), "Minor Movement", "DynoPan Right", "One Bar Wide". EchoBoy —
Ping-Pong, 1/4 note, feedback 0.52, saturation 3.72, "Edited Style" (most slots); Single 1/4 + Studio Tape.

## 1. Little AlterBoy → Voice Shift (new engine) [EXEC trial]
Parameter list: Pitch −12…+12 (0.1), Formant −12…+12, Drive 0…10, Mix 0…100 %, ShiftMode Transpose / Quantize /
Robot, FormantLink, Bypass. Latency reported 2417 samples at 48 kHz. Measurements (harmonic tones, 48 kHz):
Transpose +2.5 st: 200 → 231 Hz; Quantize: 210 → 208 Hz (G#3); Robot at Pitch 0: 262 Hz (C4), +3 st: 311 Hz; Mix 50 %
with no wet: −6.0 dB (linear); Drive at −18 dBFS 1 kHz: H1 −20.6 / −16.3 / −12.6 / −11.9 / −15.5 dB at 0 / 2.5 / 5 /
7.5 / 10 (H2 −36 dBc at 0, H3 dominant near 10). LYNX: `core/VoiceShift.{h,cpp}` — YIN + TD-PSOLA, the three modes
(Quantize = nearest semitone with a 2 ms glide; Robot = MIDI 60 + Pitch), Link = formants with the pitch, a tube drive
stage (Ember's biased tanh, 1.75 dB / step, make-down above 6), linear mix with the dry delayed by the latency; layout
`voiceShiftLayout()` (Pitch / Formant with read-outs and Link, Mode, Drive / Mix); 7 factory presets; substitute
`[soundtoys-littlealterboy]` (VST2 `StLa`).

## 2. Decapitator → Ember Saturator [EXEC trial]
12 parameters: Style A/E/N/T/P, Drive 0…10, Punish, LowCut 20 Hz…1 kHz, Tone ±12, HighCut 1…20 kHz, Mix, AutoGain,
LowThump, HighSlope Gentle / Steep, OutputTrim −24…0; latency 62. Note: parameter changes reach its audio only after
the host pumps messages (the measurements pump 300 ms after each change). Results: drive ≈ 3.2 dB / step at −18 dBFS,
−2 dB at Drive 0; Auto on / off identical audio (it moves the Output knob in the GUI); Punish ≈ +20 dB before the
drive; Low Cut 12 dB/oct, Thump +3.5 dB at the cutoff; gentle High Cut ≈ 6 dB/oct, Steep ≈ 30 dB/oct; Tone is a
pre-drive tilt (+12: −18 dB at 30 Hz, +3 dB at 16 kHz); styles at Drive 5: E brightest top, N darker top, T thinner
bass, P bass bump. LYNX (appended Ember parameters, defaults = old sound): Punish, Thump (low cut Q 1.6), Steep (5th-order
high cut), Voice (five one-click voicings — a new "console" curve for E — with their own broad shelves), Wide cuts
(+ Low cut (wide) 20 Hz–1 kHz, High cut (wide) 1–20 kHz, 6 dB/oct), the 0–10 drive read-out; editor: the strip on top
(voicing row + switches), IN / OUT meters, Output on the band panel; 5 "Voicings" factory presets; her states translated
(§0; mapped with LYNX's auto gain off, her trim − 2 dB).
