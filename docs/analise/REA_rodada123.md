## 1. SSL E-Channel → Ridge Strip "E console" voicing

**EQ and filters** [EXEC]. Impulse responses at −40 dBFS with dynamics bypassed: the flat state is a 1-sample delay;
every band at every setting is exactly one biquad (Steiglitz-McBride fits −100 … −190 dB), the high-pass is a
3rd-order Butterworth (a real pole and a Q = 1 pair at one frequency), the low-pass one biquad with complex zeros, and
the whole EQ is the product of its bands (−70 dB). Her whole EQ state: 4 bands + HP. The bands are not textbook
shapes (gain changes the bandwidth asymmetrically, the regime changes at ±7.5 dB, the Q dial has its own taper), so
LYNX keeps a calibration: per band, the analog-domain coefficients (inverse bilinear at 48 kHz) normalised by a
reference frequency, measured on a grid — 21 gains (1.5 dB) × 11 frequencies × 12 Q for LMF / HMF, 21 gains × 21
frequencies × shelf / bell for LF / HF — plus the displayed-value → frequency laws (401-point sweeps) and the HP / LP
laws (src/core/VoiceMatchTables.inc, generated by gentables.py). Interpolation: cubic in gain inside the four gain
segments, linear in log frequency and in Q dial position. Other sample rates: the prototype is rescaled so each band
keeps its frequency [model].

and her take, for 56 compressor settings (7 thresholds × 8 ratios incl. her −1.3 / 1.8 rows), 14 release / attack
settings and her states, fitted per setting by a lab model (dynfit.cpp, Levenberg-Marquardt on the per-block
